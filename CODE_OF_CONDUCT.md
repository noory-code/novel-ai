# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and internationally accessible open-source community.
Participants must communicate constructively, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and critique
ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and
personal attacks are not acceptable in repository spaces or project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity,
impact, and whether behavior continues after a clear request to stop.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact options on the
[`noory-code`](https://github.com/noory-code) organization profile. Do not open a public issue that
reveals private information about the people involved.
